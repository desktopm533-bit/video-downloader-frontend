About VidSaver

A simple, fast way to save the videos you love — built for people who just want their download to work.

Who we are

VidSaver is an independent web tool that helps people download publicly shared Instagram and TikTok videos to their own device. It was built to solve one small, everyday problem: saving a video you found online usually means using a clunky app, an intrusive extension, or a page full of pop-up ads.

Instead, VidSaver keeps things simple — paste a link, preview the video, choose a quality, and download. No account, no app install, no clutter.

What we offer

  • HD downloads for public Instagram Reels, posts and videos, with no watermark.
  • HD downloads for public TikTok videos.
  • A choice of video quality/resolution when available.
  • A clean, ad-light interface that works on both desktop and mobile browsers.

Our approach

We built VidSaver to be lightweight and respectful of your time and privacy. We don't ask you to log in with your Instagram or TikTok account, and we don't store the videos you download on our servers any longer than it takes to process your request. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

We also expect VidSaver to be used responsibly. It's meant for saving content you own, or content you have permission to reuse — full guidelines are in our Terms & Conditions.

Get in touch

Have feedback, found a bug, or have a copyright concern about content processed through VidSaver? Reach out any time at support@vidsaver.site.
