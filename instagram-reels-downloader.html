How to download an Instagram Reel

Saving a Reel with VidSaver takes under a minute and works on Android, iPhone and desktop browsers.

  1. Open the Reel in Instagram, tap the Share (paper-plane) icon and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the box above and press Fetch Video.
  3. Pick a quality from the list. The Reel downloads to your device.

On iPhone, open VidSaver in Safari so the file goes to your Downloads folder. On Android, Chrome saves it straight to your gallery or Downloads.

What you can download

  • Public Instagram Reels
  • Public feed videos (video posts on public profiles)
  • Reels shared through instagram.com/reel/ and instagram.com/p/ links

Private accounts, Stories from private profiles and deleted posts can't be fetched. See the FAQ for details.

Reel not downloading? Check these

  • The account must be public. Open the link in a private browser window; if it asks you to log in, VidSaver can't fetch it.
  • Copy the link again from the Share menu. Links cut off by a messaging app often stop working.
  • Remove tracking text after the ? in the link, then try again.
  • If the first fetch takes long, wait a few seconds and retry — our server may be waking up.
