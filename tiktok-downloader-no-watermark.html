How to download a TikTok video without watermark

  1. In TikTok, open the video, tap Share and then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the box above and press Fetch Video.
  3. Choose a quality. The video saves without the moving TikTok logo.

Works in any browser on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. No app install is needed.

Supported TikTok links

  • Full links: tiktok.com/@username/video/...
  • Short links: vm.tiktok.com/... and tiktok.com/t/...

Only public videos can be downloaded. Private or removed videos will show an error.

Why save a TikTok without the watermark?

A clean copy is handy for offline viewing, for keeping your own uploads in original quality, or for reusing your own clips in edits on other apps. If the video isn't yours, ask the creator for permission and give credit.

Troubleshooting

  • Copy the link again with the Share button instead of the address bar.
  • Make sure the video is public and still online.
  • Try a different browser if the download doesn't start.
  • Wait a few seconds and retry if the server is slow.
