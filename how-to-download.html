Quick Guide

How to Download Videos

Three simple steps to save any public Instagram or TikTok video in HD — no app install, no sign up.

Instagram Reels & Videos

1

Copy the video link

Open the Instagram app or website, find the Reel, post or video you want, tap the three-dot menu (⋯) and select "Copy Link".

2

Paste it into VidSaver

Come back to VidSaver, paste the link into the input box on the home page and tap "Fetch Video".

3

Choose a quality and download

Once the preview loads, pick the resolution you want and tap Download. Your file will save to your device in a few seconds.

TikTok Videos

1

Share the video

Open the TikTok video, tap "Share", then "Copy Link" (or copy the link straight from your browser's address bar).

2

Paste and fetch

Paste the link on the VidSaver home page and tap "Fetch Video" to load the preview and available qualities.

3

Download

Select your preferred quality and the video downloads directly — no watermark, no waiting.

Tips for a smooth download

  • Only public videos can be fetched — private accounts and private videos aren't supported.
  • If a link doesn't work, double-check that you copied the full URL, including "https://".
  • Downloaded videos are saved to your device's default downloads folder or photo gallery, depending on your browser settings.
  • Always make sure you have the right to download and reuse the content — see our Terms & Conditions.
