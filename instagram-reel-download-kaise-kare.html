Instagram Reels Downloader kholo

Android par

Chrome mein VidSaver kholo. Download ke baad video aksar Gallery ya Files → Downloads mein mil jati hai. Agar na mile to Chrome ke menu mein Downloads dekho.

iPhone par

Safari use karo (Chrome se kabhi kabhi masla aata hai). Download ke baad Safari ke address bar ke paas download arrow dabao, video ko Files mein kholo, phir Share → Save Video se Photos mein bhej do.

PC / Laptop par

Kisi bhi browser mein VidSaver kholo. Video Downloads folder mein save hogi.
