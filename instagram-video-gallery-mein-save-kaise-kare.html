Instagram Video Gallery Mein Save Kaise Kare

Download ke baad video ko gallery ya Photos mein laane ka poora tareeqa.

Pehle video download karo

Reel ka link copy karke VidSaver mein paste karo aur download dabao. Phir apne phone ke hisaab se neeche wale steps dekho.

Android — Gallery mein

  1. Chrome ka menu (⋮) kholo aur Downloads dabao.
  2. Video par tap karke dekh lo ke wo chal rahi hai.
  3. Agar Gallery mein nahi dikh rahi to Files → Downloads mein jao, video ko long-press karo aur Move se DCIM ya Movies folder mein daal do.

iPhone — Photos mein

  1. Safari mein download ke baad address bar ke paas download icon dabao.
  2. Video kholo, phir Share icon dabao.
  3. Save Video chuno. Video Photos app mein aa jayegi.

Agar video na mile

  • Browser ki storage permission check karo.
  • Phone mein jagah (storage) khali hai ya nahi dekho.
  • Dusra browser (Chrome/Safari) try karo.

Instagram video gallery mein kyun nahi aati?

Kya iPhone par bhi ho sakta hai?

Ji haan, Safari use karo aur Save Video se Photos mein save karo.
