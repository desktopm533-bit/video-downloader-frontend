Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026. Your privacy matters to us — here's exactly what we collect and why.

1Information we collect

When you use VidSaver, we process the following:

  • Video links you submit — used only to fetch the requested video's details and download file.
  • Basic technical data — such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (country/city level) and IP address, collected automatically for security and to keep the Service running smoothly.
  • Usage data — general analytics such as pages visited and features used, to help us understand and improve the Service.

We do not require you to create an account, and we never ask for your Instagram or TikTok login credentials.

2How we use this information

  • To process your video download requests.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse (for example, rate-limiting excessive requests).
  • To understand how the Service is used so we can improve performance and features.
  • To display relevant advertising through third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense (see Section 4).

3Video processing & storage

Videos you request are fetched temporarily on our backend to generate your download. We do not permanently store, index, or share the videos you download, and processed files are removed from our servers on a regular, automated basis.

4Cookies & advertising

VidSaver may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working correctly and to serve ads through third-party networks, including Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or by visiting www.aboutads.info to opt out of participating vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

5Third-party services

We rely on third-party infrastructure (such as hosting and content-delivery providers) to operate VidSaver. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses as part of delivering the Service, under their own privacy policies.

6Data security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the data we process. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7Children's privacy

VidSaver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8Your choices

You can clear cookies through your browser settings at any time, and can choose not to submit a video link if you'd prefer not to have it processed.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision.

10Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@vidsaver.site.
